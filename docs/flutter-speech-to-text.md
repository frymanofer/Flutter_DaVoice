# Flutter Speech To Text On Device

If you are searching for **Flutter speech to text**, **Flutter STT**, **Flutter ASR**, or **offline speech to text for Flutter**, this repository shows how speech recognition fits into a broader on-device voice experience.

The example app transitions into speech recognition after [wake-word detection](https://davoice.io/wake-word) and can optionally enforce the onboarded [speaker](https://davoice.io/speaker-recognition). It also demonstrates a practical pause between stages so wake word and ASR do not fight over the audio pipeline.

This is especially useful for teams building:

- voice assistants
- hands-free app flows
- healthcare voice workflows
- field-service apps
- privacy-sensitive mobile products
